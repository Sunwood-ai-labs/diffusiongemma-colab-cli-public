# Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Technical operations log video with a dark graphite canvas, precise grid structure, terminal-green progress accents, and restrained blue highlights. It should feel like a runtime trace being inspected by an engineer, not a marketing reel.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#0b0f14`
- Panel: `#121922`
- Panel border: `#263241`
- Text primary: `#f2f6fb`
- Text secondary: `#9aa8b8`
- Accent green: `#3ddc97`
- Accent blue: `#60a5fa`
- Warning amber: `#f3b84b`

## Typography

- UI: `Inter`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Mono: `JetBrains Mono`, `Menlo`, monospace

## What NOT to Do

- No neon purple/blue gradient background.
- No marketing hero layout.
- No fake success claim about answer quality.
- No tiny unreadable terminal text.
